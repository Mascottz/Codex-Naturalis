03 / A shape from chance

The chaos game

A point starts at the center, chooses a corner at random, and jumps halfway there. Repetition draws a Sierpiński triangle from almost nothing.

Points: 0 / 20,000Halfway, every timeEach jump halves the distance to one corner.
20,000

Each update contracts the distance to the chosen corner by exactly one half.
